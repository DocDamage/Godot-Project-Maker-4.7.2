# Implementation Notes

## Decisions

* The git repository root is `F:\Godot Project Maker` (the GitHub repo). The spec lives in
  `docs/spec/` (M11 T11.08 removed the duplicate `Implementation Plan and Specs/` folder).
* Local development uses Python 3.12 in `.venv` and Godot 4.7.1 (installed locally;
  4.7.2 is the validated version, so ENV-003 warnings are expected locally).
* T8.06 blob layout assumption: the 47-tile "Godot/Tilesetter" 12x4 sheet is read row-major with the 47 normalized 8-neighbor masks ascending (N=1, NE=2, E=4, SE=8, S=16, SW=32, W=64, NW=128; corner bit only with both sides); the 48th cell stays empty. 16-tile corner sets (4x4) use index bits TL=1, TR=2, BR=4, BL=8. Sheets in another order are not recognized (TILE-006). TILE-006 on a failed blob candidate is only reported when the path hints at terrain (blob, wang, autotile, terrain, corner) or the border match reached 50%, so ordinary 4x4 or 12x4 sheets stay silent. Generated A2 atlas assets use the sha256 of their pixels (not of the PNG bytes, which depend on the zlib version). The Tiled adapter output still records wangid; catalog.assemble rewrites it into {set, terrain, peering} in the detected facets (wangid is dropped there).

The reader was run over the pack's twelve files by hand (frame counts 1, 16 and 168, tags, a round trip through `write`), and the tests use files `write` makes, with unknown chunks put in by hand. (3) The pack's portal folder holds two exports under one stem (`PORTAL1` … `16` and `PORTAL 2` … `17`), so its numbers are not unique and it stays 31 pictures.

T16.21, fourth part — a bundle of packs, and sheets named by a number. **The bundle.** `pack_sniff.find_bundle` reads the names and sizes of a listing: ten or more folders with pictures, side by side at the top (inside the one folder an archive wraps everything in), no picture beside them. `PackSniff.bundle` lists them (name, path, pictures, bytes) and no file is opened: for a bundle the format question is left to the moment a folder is added. A folder's listing gives sizes through `os.scandir`, a 7z through `list()`. **One condition the card does not have:** at least half of the folders must be named as packs are (*pack, tileset, asset, kit, bundle, collection* in the name). Without it the ten part folders of a character pack (`body`, `hair`, `torso`, …) are a bundle of ten packs. *CuteSCKR*: 78 packs, 1,073 pictures, 690 MB, told in 0.02 s. The user's `side scrollers.zip` is another thing, a zip of eight zips, and is not handled: an archive inside an archive is not a folder. **Numbered sheets.** A picture whose name is only a number, at least 256 px on its short side, in a folder or a pack whose name says *tileset* or *tiles* (or of 768 x 768 whatever the folder says) is a tile sheet (`GenericGridAdapter._numbered_sheet`): 48 px for 768 x 768, the B to E sheet of RPG Maker MV, else the measured square grid, else the largest of 48, 32 and 16 that divides it. Its tags are its folders' words without those every pack of a bundle shares (`classifier.folder_tags`: *Medieval Fantasy Town Pixel Art Tileset Pack* gives fantasy, medieval, town); the pack's own name counts as the outermost folder, for a folder added on its own. Confidence 0.6, so *needs review*, and the *Why* line starts with *the file name is only a number: read by its folder's words*. The objects the picture analysis finds are kept when its grid is the one chosen. On the real town folder: 16 tile sheets of 48 px in one tile set, where there were eight tile sheets of three cell sizes and eight unknown sprites. **Seen and not solved:** *Monder Interiors* has `Tile-A2-11.png` and `Tile-A4-11.png` of 400 x 600 and `Tile-B-12.png` of 800 x 800, RPG Maker's names on sheets that are not RPG Maker's sizes (a 50 px tile, it seems); the card's point about RPG Maker names inside other packs has to deal with that.

T16.21, fifth part — what a sprite-cutting tool left behind. `adapters/cutter.py` (`CutterAdapter`, priority 45): a `manifest/sprites.json` or `sprites.csv` (or one anywhere under a folder that starts with `_organized_sprites`) is read, the JSON before the CSV; each entry's rectangle becomes an **object of its sheet**, with the category as its tag and `authoritative` set, so the rectangles the picture analysis guesses for that sheet give way. The sheet is found by the file name in `source_file` (the JSON has it; only the name is used, the path is of the artist's own disk and reaches no catalog), else by the number in `sheet_05`: the sheet called `5.png`, else the fifth. **The card says `sheet_01` is the pack's first sheet; in the real pack the sheets are 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8 and `sheet_05` is `5.png`**, so the number is tried as a name first. The cut copies and the tool's previews are *unused* with their reason (`unused_because:cut_copy`, `cut_preview`). A manifest that does not fit (a rectangle outside its sheet, a sheet that is not there, no entry, not JSON) is **ADAPT-007**, a note; the copies are then sprites like any picture and only the previews are unused. `ADAPT-007` has `{reason}`, which the card's wording did not need. On the real supermarket folder: 7 tile sheets of 48 px, **431 objects** from the manifest (the card's number), 445 copies and previews unused.

T16.21, sixth part — one art for several engines. `classifier.engine_plan`: a pack with two or more folders under `engines/` is read from one of them, Godot's when there is one, else RPG Maker MV's, else another RPG Maker's, else the first by name; `not_this_engine` gives the reason for every picture of another engine's folder (`unused_because:other_engine`), and the pack gets the warning `engines:<n>:<kept>` for its card. **How the real pack differs from the card's picture of it:** the engine folders of *Super Retro Ranch* are not copies of each other. `assets/` holds the art as single strips, and each engine folder holds that art put together for the engine (character sheets for RPG Maker, autotiles for Godot and Unity). So nothing is `duplicate_of` a file: there is no one file a sheet repeats, and the pictures of the other engines are *unused* with their reason and no `duplicate_of` tag. Godot's folder has every autotile twice, `2x2` (64 x 64) and `3x3` (192 x 64, the 47-tile sheet the terrain reader knows): with both there, `2x2` is unused (`other_layout`), and so are the raw 16 x 96 templates under `assets/autotiles` when Godot's folder has autotiles; the card would read those templates as 6-tile sheets, for which there is no reader, and with the 47-tile sheet there it is not needed. Everything unused this way is in the catalog and comes back with a role. **Not done:** the words on the pack card (*art for 5 engines: Godot's is used*; the warning is there for T16.16), and `_<N>split` / `_<N>dir`. On the real pack: 1,040 files, 40 left out of the inventory (21 of macOS, 19 links and *In development* files), 834 assets, 335 of them unused (288 of the four other engines, 24 of the 2x2 layout, 23 raw templates), `engines:5:Godot`.

T16.21, seventh part — copies and photographs. A `.jpg` or `.jpeg` has no transparency, so the generic reader never makes a tile sheet or a numbered sheet of one: a background from 256 px, a plain picture below. A file whose name says it is a copy (`classifier.copy_of`: a suffix that is not ASCII, as `_副本`, or *copy*, *(copy)*, *- Copy*) **and** whose original lies beside it is unused with the reason; without the original beside it, it is a picture like any other.

T16.21, eighth part — colour variants a pack ships as files, in the catalog. `classifier.colour_key` gives what a file is without its colour and the colour (a colour word or two joined, as a word of the file name or as a whole folder; numbers stay, so `cow_01` and `cow_02` are two animals; `Redwood` and `greenhouse` name no colour). `catalog.mark_colour_variants` groups the pictures of one pack, size and usage that share a key and have two colours or more: every one gets `colour:<name>`, and every one but the first `variant_of:<id of the first>`; the first is the file with no colour word, else the first colour by name. `catalog.colour_variants(catalog, id)` lists them and the *Why* line says *a colour variant of …*. **This is the catalog's half of the card's point, not the whole of it.** The card wants one card in the audit with a *Variants (8)* row and a choice on the Mapping page and in the dock; here every variant is still an asset and a card of its own, and nothing in the wizard reads the tags yet (T16.16 is the wizard's card). It does not go through the variant pipeline of T13.18 either: that one renders a recolour from a source, and these files exist. So the racing pack is 224 assets, not the 32 the card's *Done when* counts: 26 vehicles with 173 colour variants between them (groups of 4 to 7), and the ranch's cows are groups of 36.

T16.21, ninth part — RPG Maker's sheet names in a pack of another kind. `rpgmaker.marked_letter`: a name that puts the engine or the word *tile* beside the sheet letter (`prison_MV_A4.png`, `Tile-A2-11.png`, `Tile-B-12.png`) **and** a picture of that sheet's size (32 px or 48 px tiles) is an RPG Maker sheet wherever it lies, and one such file is enough for the adapter to run. A letter alone (`plan_B.png`) marks nothing outside `img/tilesets`, as before. The sheets of *Monder Interiors* have the names and not the sizes (400 x 600, 800 x 800, 815 x 819) and are left to the generic reader. The card's *a 768 x 768 sheet with a 48 px grid is offered as a B to E sheet when nothing else names its grid* is done for numbered sheets only (the fourth part).
