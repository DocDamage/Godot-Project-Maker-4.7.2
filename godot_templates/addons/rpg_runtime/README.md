# rpg_runtime

Runtime addon copied into every project generated by rpggen. Classes use the `Rpg` prefix.
This folder is generated-owned: rpggen may rewrite it on regeneration. Licensed MIT-0 (see LICENSE);
the version is in `VERSION` and the history in `CHANGELOG.md`.

Autoloads, in order: `Events`, `GameState`, `SaveManager`, `SceneRouter`, `RpgSettings`, `RpgSmoke`.

## Dialogue conditions

`expr := term (("and"|"or") term)*`, `term := ["not"] atom`. Evaluated **left to right with no
precedence**, so `a or b and c` means `(a or b) and c`.

| Atom | True when |
| --- | --- |
| `flag:<entity>.<key>` | the flag is truthy |
| `item:<id>` / `item:<id>>=N` | the player holds at least 1 / N |
| `gold>=N` | the player has at least N gold |
| `level>=N` | the player's level is at least N |
| `quest:<id>=<state>` | the quest is in that state |
| `quest_active:<id>` / `quest_done:<id>` | the quest is under way / finished |
| `quest_stage:<id>:<stage>` | the quest is at that stage |
| `party:<actor id>` | the character is in the party (the player always is) |
| `var:<name><op><N>` | the variable compares so with N; `op` is `>=`, `<=`, `==`, `!=`, `>` or `<` (a variable never set is 0) |

## Dialogue events

`set_flag:<entity>.<key>=<value>`, `give_item:<id>[:qty]`, `take_item:<id>[:qty]`, `give_gold:N`,
`set_quest:<id>=<state>`, `quest_start:<id>`, `quest_stage:<id>:<stage>`, `quest_complete:<id>`,
`join_party:<actor id>` / `leave_party:<actor id>` (turn-based; see below), `heal_full` (the whole party), `open_shop:<id>` (opens `game/data/shops/<id>.tres` when the
dialogue closes), `set_var:<name>=<N>`, `add_var:<name>:<N>` (N may be negative),
`run_sequence:<id>` (plays `game/data/sequences/<id>.tres` once the dialogue has closed) and
`emit:<name>[:arg]` (sent as `Events.dialogue_event`).

## Sequences

A sequence is a cutscene: an `RpgSequence` resource (`game/data/sequences/<id>.tres`) whose
`commands` are Dictionaries such as `{"cmd": "move_actor", "entity": "town_npc_001", "tiles": [2, 0]}`.
`entity` is an `entity_id` of the current map, or `player`; distances are in tiles of the map.
The full command list with every argument is at the top of `events/sequence.gd`; in short:

| Commands | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| `move_actor`, `face`, `animate` | walk a character (x first, then y; walls do not stop it), turn it, hold an animation |
| `wait`, `fade_out`, `fade_in`, `shake` | timing and the screen (the shake follows Settings > Screen shake) |
| `camera_pan`, `camera_lock`, `camera_unlock` | move the camera away from the player and back; keep it still |
| `show_dialogue`, `play_sound` | a dialogue file or lines written into the command; a sound slot |
| `give_item`, `take_item`, `set_flag`, `set_var`, `add_var`, `event` | change the game (`event` takes any dialogue event) |
| `start_battle`, `change_map` | a battle with enemies from `game/data/enemies/` (a defeat ends the sequence); another map |
| `label`, `goto`, `if` / `else`, `call`, `end` | flow: jump on a condition, run another sequence and come back |
| `wait_for` | wait for a character (or everything) started with `"wait": false` |

Characters, fades, pans and shakes started with `"wait": false` run in the background while the
next commands go on, so two characters can walk at once. While a sequence plays, the player
cannot move or open the pause menu, autosave waits and enemies hold still. When it ends,
the screen, the camera and every held animation are put back. A sequence marked `skippable` is
skipped with cancel. The same sequence always plays the same way (it advances with the physics
frames).

Start one with an `RpgTriggerArea` (dock: Project > *Add trigger area*): a walk-in or interact
area that runs `sequence_id` (or a sequence kept in the map in its `sequence` property), with
`once` remembered by the flag `<entity_id>.triggered` and an optional `condition`. The dialogue
event `run_sequence:<id>` and `RpgSequenceRunner.instance().run(sequence)` start one too; a second
one waits until the first has finished. The project validator reports unknown commands, missing
arguments and labels, and sequences, dialogues, enemies or maps that do not exist (VAL-023).

## Party members (turn-based)

`join_party:<id>` adds the character defined in `game/data/actors/<id>.tres` (an
`RpgActorDefinition`: name, sprite frames, portrait, starting stats); `leave_party:<id>` takes them
out again and returns what they wore to the inventory. The party holds at most 4 characters, the
player included. On the map the members walk behind the player, one tile apart along the player's
path (`RpgFollowers`, pictures without a body; boot's `followers` export turns them off). In battle every member picks a command in party order, ally-targeted commands
(items) can be aimed at any standing member, and experience is shared: each member who is standing
at the end gets the full amount. A member who fell is back on their feet with 1 HP when the battle
is over. The status menu pages through the members (left / right, or its first button), and each
has their own equipment. The party is part of the save. **The action RPG has no followers in 1.0**:
the events work there, but nothing appears in the world.

## Sound

`RpgAudio` (a child of `SceneRouter`, its `audio` property) plays the **sound slots** of
the game. `rpggen/sound_bank.json` maps a slot key (`music_map`, `music_battle`, `music_title`,
`sfx_ui_move`, `sfx_ui_confirm`, `sfx_ui_cancel`, `sfx_hit`, `sfx_attack`, `sfx_pickup`,
`sfx_chest`, `sfx_door`, `sfx_step`, `sfx_save`, `sfx_levelup`) to a resource path. `play(slot)`,
`play_ui_slot(slot)` and `play_music_slot(slot)` do nothing, and log nothing, for a slot that is
not set. Game events (`item_added`, `player_damaged`, `level_up`, `game_saved`, `chest_opened`,
`door_used`, `attack_started`) are wired to their slots; the autosave (slot 0) is silent.

## Saving and game over

`SaveManager` has slots 1 to 3 and the autosave slot 0 (`AUTOSAVE_SLOT`); `latest_save_slot()` of
`RpgDefeatPolicy` is the newest of them, or -1. `RpgDefeatPolicy.show_game_over()` shows
`RpgGameOver` (continue, try again, quit to title) and carries the choice out; smoke runs and a
combat mode with an `auto_choose` hook skip the screen and take the default (the newest save,
else the start of the map with full health).

## Credits

`RpgCredits` (`data/credits.gd`) is the data of the Credits screen: `packs` (one
`{name, author, license, license_url, url, attribution_required}` per asset pack) and `extra`
(`{section, text}` rows). The builder's `credits_recipe` writes it to
`generated/data/credits.tres` on every build and seeds `game/data/credits_extra.tsv` once.
`extra_lines()` reads the .tsv when the project has it and the baked rows otherwise.
`RpgCreditsMenu` (`ui/credits_menu.gd`, scene `generated/scenes/ui/credits_menu.tscn`) lists your
lines by section, the packs and the engine; up and down scroll, Back or `cancel` closes it. The
title screen and the pause menu open it from their *Credits* button.

## Debug overlay

In debug builds with the debug tools feature on, **F3** shows FPS, map, position, tile and flag
count, and a console: `tp <spawn_id>`, `give <item_id> [qty]`, `gold <n>`, `flag <entity>.<key>=<v>`,
`heal`, `encounter`, `collision`, `help`.

## Archetypes

Each archetype lives in `archetypes/<name>/`; a generated project contains only the one it was
generated with. The shared code never refers to an archetype's classes: boot adds the archetype's
combat mode (`SceneRouter.combat`, an `RpgCombatMode`) and shared code only calls its contract.

### turn_based

An overworld enemy (`RpgOverworldEnemy`, a Contact area on layer `triggers`) builds an
`RpgEncounterData` and calls `SceneRouter.combat.start_encounter(data)`. `RpgTurnBasedMode` freezes
the player, pushes `generated/scenes/battle/battle.tscn` and, when the battle is over,
`SceneRouter.pop_scene(result)` brings the map back. Then the mode applies the result: VICTORY sets
the flag `<entity_id>.defeated` (so `RpgPersistent` frees the enemy on every map load), ESCAPED
disables the enemy's contact for 1.5 s, DEFEAT returns to the newest save, or to the map's default
spawn with full health when there is none.

The battle is `RpgBattleController`, a state machine
(`INIT > ROUND_START > PLAYER_CHOOSE > ENEMY_CHOOSE > EXECUTE > CHECK > VICTORY | DEFEAT | ESCAPED > END`)
that knows no nodes. Commands are `RpgBattleCommand` resources (`game/data/battle_commands/`):
`attack`, `defend` (priority 10, halves damage until the next round), `item`, `skill` and `run`.
Damage, hit, crit, magic, heal, element and escape formulas are pure functions in `RpgBattleMath`;
what an attack or a skill does to a battler (log entries) is `RpgBattleEffects`; the order of a
round is `RpgTurnManager.order` (priority, speed with haste and slow, player side, id; never
random). All randomness comes from `GameState.rng`, so a seeded battle is repeatable.

Skills are `RpgSkillDefinition` resources (`game/data/skills/<id>.tres`, loaded by the controller
into `skills`). The player side knows what its `RpgActorDefinition.skills` lists for its level and
pays `mp_cost` from `RpgStats.mp`; an enemy with `max_mp` 0 casts for free. Elements scale damage
by the target's rate (`RpgBattleActor.element_rate`: the definition and the worn items, the lowest
listed rate; 0 immune, negative absorbs). Statuses (`RpgBattleStatus.TABLE`) live in
`RpgBattleActor.statuses` (status -> rounds left, 0 = the whole battle) and end with the battle;
poison and regen tick and every status counts down after each round.

Enemy AI: the rules of `behavior.turn_based_rules` first (`RpgBattleAi`: the first rule whose
`when` holds and whose `chance` is rolled is used; `once` rules run once a battle), then a heal
item from `behavior.items` below 25% HP, else a roll of `behavior.turn_based`
(`{"attack": 1.0, "defend": 0.0}`; a key may be a skill id) at a random living player-side
battler. What it cannot do (a skill without the MP, nobody to aim at) becomes an attack.

Tests and the smoke run set `controller.auto_choose` (or `SceneRouter.combat.auto_choose` for the
next battle): it is called as `auto_choose.call(actor, battle)` and returns
`{command_id, target_index}` (plus `item_id` or `skill_id`); no UI is needed then.

Random encounters: an `RpgEncounterZone` (an `Area2D` whose `CollisionPolygon2D` /
`CollisionShape2D` children are its shape, or the painted cells of a TileMapLayer: `cells_layer`,
else a layer named `Encounters`, which is hidden in the game) points at an `RpgEncounterTable`
(`game/data/encounters/<id>.tres`: `groups` = [{enemies: [<enemy ids>], weight}], `can_escape`).
The mode counts the tiles the player walks (`RpgEncounterSteps`; a jump of more than two tiles is
a door, not walking) and at every step inside an enabled zone draws from `GameState.rng`: one in
`steps_per_encounter` steps brings a battle with a group drawn by weight. Every battle (random or
not) is followed by 6 grace steps without one, and nothing is counted during a map change, a
battle or a sequence. A zone is off while its `disabled_flag` (`<entity>.<key>`) is true. A random
battle defeats nothing on the map. The dock's Project tab has *Add encounter zone (over the
selection)*; the validator reports zones without a table, with unknown enemies or without a shape
(VAL-024). The action RPG has no random encounters: its enemies stand on the map (spawners are not
part of 1.0).

### arpg

Combat happens on the map. `RpgArpgMode` (`SceneRouter.combat`) never has an encounter
(`is_in_combat()` is false); it owns the defeat policy (`apply_defeat()`, the same as the
turn-based one), the quick item (`quick_item()`, `set_quick_item(id)`, `use_quick_item()`; the
choice is the flag `arpg_hud.quick_item`) and the "Set quick item" button it adds to the inventory
menu (`customize_inventory_menu(menu)`).

Damage primitives: `RpgHitbox` (an `Area2D` that is live only while `active`; hits each hurtbox once
per activation, polls overlaps because a hurtbox that is already inside gives no `area_entered`),
`RpgHurtbox` (`receive(info)`, an invulnerability window: player 0.6 s, enemies 0.15 s),
`RpgHealth` (the numbers live in the actor's `RpgStats`; `died` fires once) and `RpgDamageInfo`.
Layers: player hitbox 7 (mask 10), enemy hitbox 8 (mask 9), player hurtbox 9, enemy hurtbox 10.
`RpgArpgMath`: `max(1, attack - defense / 2)`, x1.5 on a crit, one `GameState.rng` draw per roll.

`RpgPlayerCombat` is the player's state machine (`IDLE`, `MOVE`, `ATTACK`, `HURT`, `DODGE`,
`DEAD`; a generic `RpgStateMachine`). It uses the actor's `move_locked`, `facing_locked` and
`animation_locked` flags so the input and the automatic walk / idle animations do not fight it.
The hitbox is live on the active frames of `attack_<facing>` (SpriteFrames meta
`active_frames_<anim>`, else the middle third); without an attack animation `RpgSwingEffect` plays
the generated arc and the hitbox is live 0.08 to 0.2 s after the press.

`RpgEnemyAi` is only the state machine of an enemy (`IDLE`, `WANDER`, `ALERT`, `CHASE`, `ATTACK`,
`HURT`, `STUNNED`, `DEAD`); `Detection`, `Chase`, `Wander`, `EnemyAttack`, `EnemyShoot`,
`EnemyCharge` and `LootDrop` are components under `Components` (the shooting and charging ones are
made in `_ready` when the kind needs them). Their numbers are the keys of `definition.behavior`
(see `RpgEnemyAi.DEFAULT_BEHAVIOR`); `behavior.kind` is `melee`, `shooter` (keeps `keep_away_px`,
fires from `shoot_range_px`), `turret` (never moves) or `charger` (dashes with its hitbox live,
then recovers).

`RpgProjectile` is a shot made in code (`create(shooter, from, direction, team, speed, range)`): the
layers of its team's hitbox plus the world, the melee damage formula with the shooter's attack when
it was fired, gone after one hit, a wall or its range. A worn weapon with `RpgItemDefinition.ranged`
makes the player's attack fire one arrow (`RpgPlayerCombat.fire_arrow`) instead of the swing. A dead enemy sets the flag `<entity_id>.defeated` (an
`RpgPersistent` child frees it on every map load), emits `entity_defeated`, gives experience and gold
and drops `RpgPickup`s. `RpgArpgHud` is the HUD scene `hud_arpg.tscn`.
